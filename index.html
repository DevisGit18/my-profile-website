AI in Robotic Surgery

Research Attachment, BDF Royal Medical Services, Bahrain · 2026–Ongoing

Supervised by Dr. Ayman Raees (Robotic Surgery Programme Director, BDF Hospital), facilitated by Prof. Martin Corbally (RCSI Bahrain)

Proposed and conducting independent IRB-approved research (RMS-MH/IRB/2026-1180) applying deep learning to Hugo RAS robotic surgical video. Scope includes development of a surgical-video dataset for instrument detection and localisation, deep learning-based perception using YOLOv8/SAM2, surgical learning-curve quantification from Touch Surgery Enterprise telemetry, and warm ischaemia time decomposition.
